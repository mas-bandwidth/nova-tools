The matching stable tools release for nova sprint v1.5.4: a hotfix to the sandbox wall's caps.

Install: download the assets for your platform from this release, then run `nova-sprint adopt <version> --source <nova-tools checkout at v1.5.3> --sprint-release <the nova-sprint v1.5.4 release downloaded> --inventory <file> --reason <text>` (see docs/SPEC-RELEASE.md).

## What changed in v1.5.3

- The `nova-sandbox` bare form sizes its caps from the machine: 64 processes per logical CPU and at least 512, never more than a quarter of the cgroup TasksMax above it; and an eighth of the machine's memory, never more than a quarter of the cgroup MemoryMax above it, at least 8 GiB and never more than half the machine (8 GiB where the memory cannot be read). Four trees at their caps fit under the member login slice wherever a quarter of the slice is 8 GiB or more (not on 31 GiB members, where the 8 GiB floor wins), so the slice's OOM killer does not take a sibling worker. The v1.5.1 sizing keyed both caps on 32 CPUs, so 16-thread members with 62 to 125 GiB got 512 processes and 8 GiB, and on 2026-10-17 the wall still ended about half the worker runs on hetzner1, hetzner2 and amd-3 (`runaway: 714 processes (cap 512)`, `8714186752 bytes of memory (cap 8589934592)`).
- Linux: the per-user thread limit (RLIMIT_NPROC) set for a walled child leaves room for every slot of the bench at its cap (4 slots, 8 threads a process, never above the slice TasksMax). It was the task count at the start plus twice the cap, so a child started on a quiet bench ran out of threads as the other slots grew: the exit 134s (Go newosproc, pthread_create failed).
- Explicit `--max-procs` and `--max-mem` still win, and the `run` verb's caps are unchanged. No wire or record change; going back to v1.5.2 is a binary swap.
