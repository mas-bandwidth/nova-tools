# nova-fuse READ and USE rating, nova-tools 1.2.0

Rater: qwen3.8-flash in opencode, a worker dealt this friend's re-rate card; the read and the runs below are this worker's own
Build: 6ce37744efd4
READ: 8/10
USE: 8.5/10

Question: is this a good tool for an AI to use?

## Reasons

READ. `nova-fuse help` opens with one sentence of purpose, a five-line how-it-works naming the box, the lockdown and the quarantine, the fail-closed rule ("no box, or a broken one, reads as blown"), and the honest limit ("The tool enforces nothing: your harness runs check first"). The usage block names every verb with its effect; the exit-code paragraph is the truth table; the box JSON shape is printed; the `example:` block is a six-command sitting a stranger runs in order. `nova-fuse help <verb>` gives each verb a usage line, an effect class (inspection, local write, or the refused-by-design third), its flags, and its own exit codes. Every `-h` after a verb is refused at exit 2 with the reason (exit 0 is this tool's CLEAR) and the pointer to `help <verb>` — the one exception in the set, stated three times where it matters. That is readable in minutes, and the banner is true of the binary almost line for line. What keeps it from 10 is that the normative spec still disagrees with the binary's bytes (the failure token, the status order in one of two self-contradicting paragraphs), the banner's lockdown-replacement recipe contradicts `help lift lockdown` and the spec on what preserves the standing quarantines, the two verb lists a refusal prints disagree with each other, and the serialization the code actually performs is absent from the spec's write paragraph.

USE. Read cold, then used for real in a throwaway directory: `init` made the box at 0644 and verified it by re-reading; the six-line sitting ran as printed, exit codes and all; `check --box <b> a-forum` after a `quarantine` exited 1 with the lift command embedded, pasted as printed, and worked. `lift lockdown` refused in three lines at exit 2 before reading anything, `--box ./lock.json` absent included. `--dry-run` on all four writing verbs planned without writing, `dry_run=true`, including a `lockdown` that would make a box at a path with none. Refusals named every problem at once (a bare `nova-fuse quarantine` printed the missing `--box`, the hint, and the missing surface-and-reason in one run), the near-miss `quarantin` got "did you mean quarantine?", a repeated `--box`, a dash-led `--box` value, a late flag, and a non-numeric `--max` were each refused at 2 with what the flag wants. Fail-closed held under every probe: a misspelled `lokdown` key, a bare `null`, a corrupt file, a directory, a symlink, a mode-000 box all answer 2, never clear; `quarantine` refuses to narrow an unreadable box and `lockdown` blows anyway after moving the old bytes to `<box>.unreadable`, announcing it on a NOTE line. Folded matching cuts both ways as documented (`Discord` quarantined answers `FUSE FAILED` to `check discord`; `lift DISCORD` takes it), a hand-edited reason with an embedded newline arrives escaped on one line, a displayed `\x20` spelling retyped into `check` refuses instead of clearing, and `status --max 2` of three surfaces printed the uncapped count, two lines and one `STATUS MORE` with the remedy. The four write races of 1.1.0 are fixed: two concurrent `quarantine` runs on one fresh box both landed under the box lock, and a second `quarantine` of a standing surface keeps the first stamp and reason and says so. A 10 would print the bytes its own spec names, put the standing quarantine count on the lockdown failure line, resolve the two verb lists and the two replacement recipes into one each, and say in help that the box is world-readable.

Not tried: nothing was run against a live store or a server; every run was in a scratch directory under the job. `NOVA_FUSE_BOX` was probed and, as pinned, is not consulted.

## Good, keep

Fail-closed is total and tested-shaped: absent box, unreadable box, misspelled key, wrong JSON shape, symlink, directory and mode-000 all answer 2 with the fact named, and `quarantine` refuses to narrow an unreadable or absent box while `lockdown` blows anyway after preserving the bytes as evidence. Writes verify by re-reading, serialize under the box lock, and re-blowing or re-quarantining one fuse keeps the standing record and says the new reason was not recorded. Refusals name every independent problem in one run, the gate's failure line carries the pasteable lift remedy (correct through spaces in the box path and a dash-led surface after `--`), and folded surface matching holds in both directions with every removal announced under its stored spelling.
