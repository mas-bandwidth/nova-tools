## Sprint generation record

The banner is 2172 × 724 pixels. Its final generation output is
`exec-615f4f6b-a85d-4856-9624-e9125ff54ff1.png`. It follows composition and
expression studies from the original
`exec-26f79342-e16a-4077-bb12-70a9b4f16077.png`, retaining the sunny park,
exact “Nova Sprint” title, star, colors and robot identities. The final edit
brief specifies a straight 50 m sprint, five distinct lanes, left-to-right
movement, one runner in each lane, different stride phases, and individual
emotional states. Follow-up edits separate the orange and blue lanes and lift
the purple runner's stride clear of its lane divider. Its SHA-256 is
`6fd2fb55d8261871e5bc08da98ba0eae9236a231bd15e763c719375eabd3c1f4`.

`exec-f1f96466-c18e-471d-825f-d468096d2181.png`; its brief requested the blue
runner's silhouette, expressive face, golden star and clear margins, without
text or a background. Its SHA-256 is
`8a7f76b153046fd3879b2db9a5fcd90a0d7eba8b3bd22c9bf2c7ce0abb74310a`.
