CARD-8008 — nova-bus: send refuses a fresh clone that wrote .nova-bus/defaults

Issue: `inbox` demands `.nova-bus/defaults` (or the env var) for
--receipt-max-words; once the clone writes that file, `send` refuses over it as
"changes that are not this note". The two verbs must agree: .nova-bus/ is the
tool's own per-clone state, never a note. Fixed by making the clean guard ignore
.nova-bus/ (internal/bus EnsureClean).
